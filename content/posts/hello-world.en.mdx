## Why this site

I keep a pile of notes — design decisions, gotchas, half-finished ideas — that don't really fit in a Twitter thread or a commit message. This blog is where some of those will live.

Expect short pieces on:

- **Backend reliability**: Go services, gRPC contracts, PostgreSQL gotchas (especially geospatial).
- **Frontend ergonomics**: tiny Next.js patterns I keep reaching for, mistakes I keep making.
- **The seam between them**: typed contracts, generated clients, error contracts that survive a refactor.

## What this isn't

A linkdump. There are better places for that. If I post a piece here, I'm trying to make a small claim and back it up.

Thanks for reading.
