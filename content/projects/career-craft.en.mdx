## Overview

Career Craft helps students explore careers without falling into the rabbit hole of reading a hundred articles. They pick interests, the UI surfaces matched roles with concrete next steps.

## Stack

Static React + Tailwind, no backend, fully indexed and accessible. Built during the Brain Station 23 attachment to practise shipping production-ready frontend within a sprint.

## What I built

- Filterable role grid backed by a tagged dataset.
- Saved-roles drawer (local storage) so a session is portable.
- Mobile-first — the project was designed for users on shared devices.
