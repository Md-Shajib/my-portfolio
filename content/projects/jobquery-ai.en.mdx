## Overview

JobQueryAI lets you ask natural-language questions across a corpus of recently scraped LinkedIn job listings — "show me Go roles in Dhaka with remote-friendly hours" — and get focused answers instead of a hundred tabs.

## Stack

Selenium handles the scrape, structured outputs land in JSON, **Llama 3.2** answers grounded queries through a thin Flask interface. Local-first by design — your scrape, your model, your data.

## What I built

- Resilient scraper with retry + politeness windows so the source doesn't hate you.
- Structured corpus schema (role / company / location / remote-flag / posted-at) — the corpus is searchable without the model.
- Prompt templates that bias the model toward citing source listings instead of hallucinating titles.

## Caveats

This was a study project. Run it against a corpus you control, respect platform terms of service, and don't deploy a scraper you can't stop.
